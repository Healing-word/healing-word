About Us


Untold desolation is an online literary magazine featuring fiction, poetry and personal essays by south Sudanese’s and African writers. We publish quarterly stories with selected themes inspiring our thoughtful readers with deeper understanding of unheard challenges facing our societies.

Our Mission


To bring into light the unheard voice, overlooked tales and unspoken experience through personal essay, poetry and short fictions by south Sudanese and African writers. We inspire our readers with untold stories that need thoughtful attention.
