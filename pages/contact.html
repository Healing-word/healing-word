Submission Guidelines


Before submission, please keep in mind the following:


1. The theme for this quarter is the loneliness (how do we feel when we lost something that is precise to us?).


1. We are interested in story/poem that is originally written by you.

2. Work written through Ai is prohibited.

3. We do not consider already publish work, including work appear online, or on social media.

4. Simultaneous submissions is allow. But if your work is accepted elsewhere, notify us as soon as possible.

5. We prefer work that are short, i.e for poem between 50-300 words and for fiction/ nonfiction between 1500-3500 words

7. Author fulname, country, city, short bio, profile picture, and story/ poem should all be included in document file that is to be submitted.

6. Please, your work is to be submitted via email.
