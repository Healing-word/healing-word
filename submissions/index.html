Call for Submissions: Nervous Stress

by Michael Kuany




For this quarter, your submission (fiction, poetry, and essay) to untold desolation should revolve around the theme: stress. While writing your work please recall the following guidelines:

1. We accept fiction (1000-3000 words), poetry (50-300 words) and nonfiction (1000-3000 words).

2. We accept simultaneous submission. We only ask you to inform us as soon as your work is accepted elsewhere.

3. We do not accept multiple submission. Just submit only to one genre.

4. we do not accept previously published works. This includes works that are published already in social media.

5. We want your original work. We also ask you to avoid plagiarisms.

6. We do not accept AI- generated work.

7. Your work must be in Microsoft word. we do not accept submission in PDF format.

8. Include your bio (30-70words) in your email submission.

9. Please, submit you work via untolddesolation@gmail.com
